- Use moss-green scales and warm-cream cloth accents.
- Add one compact cream mushroom-cap gardener cowl attached around the shoulders.
- The accessory remains attached, keeps the same shape and side, and never changes hands or disappears.
- exact master idle beats: neutral -> small inhale -> readable blink with tiny tail follow-through -> near-neutral.

At native scale Moss must be distinguishable from the other residents by palette and silhouette detail, not color alone. No extra prop, weapon, readable text, detached effect, particles, scenery, shadow patch, border, or watermark.
