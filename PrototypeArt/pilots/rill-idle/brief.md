- Use slate-blue scales and stone-gray cloth accents.
- Add one short square stone-mender apron with a tiny tool handle fixed securely at the belt.
- The accessory remains attached, keeps the same shape and side, and never changes hands or disappears.
- exact master idle beats: neutral -> small inhale -> readable blink with tiny tail follow-through -> near-neutral.

At native scale Rill must be distinguishable from the other residents by palette and silhouette detail, not color alone. No extra prop, weapon, readable text, detached effect, particles, scenery, shadow patch, border, or watermark.
