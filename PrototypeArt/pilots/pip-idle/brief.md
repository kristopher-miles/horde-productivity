## Identity invariants

- Preserve the master snout, eye, horn count and shape, hands, feet, tail, body proportions, outline family, and idle poses.
- Use teal scales and warm-gold cloth accents.
- Add one compact warm-gold lantern-keeper shoulder capelet with a small attached loop tab; no lantern and no readable list.
- The accessory remains attached, keeps the same shape and side, and never changes hands or disappears.
- exact master idle beats: neutral -> small inhale -> readable blink with tiny tail follow-through -> near-neutral.

At native scale Pip must be distinguishable from the other residents by palette and silhouette detail, not color alone. No extra prop, weapon, readable text, detached effect, particles, scenery, shadow patch, border, or watermark.
